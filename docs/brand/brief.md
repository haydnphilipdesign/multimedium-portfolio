## 1. Positioning ✅

> **Multimedium designs and builds websites for real estate professionals: transaction coordinators, agents, and brokerages.** Every site is planned, written with you, designed, and built by one person, Haydn, who grew up around the business and builds software for transaction coordinators. The result is a site that explains your services the way agents and clients think about them, looks as organized as you are, and makes it simple to hire you, start a file, or book a consultation.

**One-line version:** Websites for the people who get deals to the closing table.

**Why Multimedium (only claims we can back):**

1. **Insider knowledge.** Haydn's mom runs a transaction coordination business, and Haydn builds TC software (Norma Suite, UtilitySheet). Clients don't have to explain what a cover sheet, a file start, or a listing package is.
2. **One person, start to finish.** The person on the first call writes the plan, designs, builds, launches, and answers email afterward.
3. **Real estate work across the business.** That includes TC companies (Structured Closings, PA Real Estate Support Services, TAG) and a brokerage (Haven Compass Realty).
4. **Fast, custom, hand-built sites.** No page-builder templates, with transparent starting prices.

## 3. Voice ✅

- **Plain, specific, calm.** Write like a capable person explaining things across a desk, not an agency pitching.
- **Customer first.** Lead with the reader's situation ("Before an agent sends you a file, they look you up."), not with Multimedium.
- **Concrete over adjectives.** Prefer "Home · Services · Start a File · About" to "a tailored multi-page experience."
- **First person singular.** "I", never "we" (it's one person). Clients are "you."
- **Use:** start a file, intake, agents, buyers and sellers, brokerage, listing, closing, clear, organized, one person, plain terms.
- **Avoid:** elevate, empower, seamless, cutting-edge, world-class, next chapter, work as hard as you do, unlock, game-changer, "studio" bravado, guarantees, and unexplained jargon ("component library", "conversion funnel").
- **Headings** state something useful. No poetic eyebrow labels ("Your next chapter"). The serif italic accent appears at most once per page (the hero).
- **Arrows:** → for internal links, ↗ only for links that leave multimedium.dev.

## 4. Naming ✅

- **Multimedium**: one word, capital M only. The lockup is the "m." badge + "multimedium." wordmark, used the same way in nav and footer.
- **Norma**: the core TC workspace (transactions, tasks, deadlines, documents). Status: **private beta, invite-only, free during beta.**
- **Norma Suite**: the product family: Norma, Norma Intake, Norma Reports, and UtilitySheet.
- **Norma Intake**: collects transaction details and documents and structures incoming data. Status: **live, self-serve, free tier (5 intakes/mo)** (intake.normatc.com).
- **Norma Reports**: agent-facing transaction progress reports. Status: ⚠️ not publicly launched. Describe it as part of the suite's direction, not as a shipped feature.
- **UtilitySheet**: collects seller utility details and produces branded utility sheets. It keeps its own brand and domain. Status: **live, free tier (3 sheets/mo), paid Pro/Teams** (utilitysheet.com).
- **NormaTC**: the older name, still the domain (normatc.com). Mention it once where needed: "Norma (formerly NormaTC)."
- **Client Portal** (portal.multimedium.dev): ⚠️ status and audience unconfirmed. Leave it off the site until Haydn decides.
- Always distinguish **what exists today** from **where the suite is headed**.

## 5. Proof rules ✅ (non-negotiable)

1. **Never invent** clients, testimonials, names, statistics, logos, awards, or results, including in demos, mockups, placeholders, or "illustrative" content on multimedium.dev.
2. **Concept or fictional work is never shown as proof.** There is no Lab and there are no demo sites.
3. **Client quotes** need the client's written permission and are shown with name, role, and company. Collect them through `/review`, which records consent.
4. **Testimonials about a client's own business** (e.g., Chelsea Daley's Google reviews, Structured Closings' client quotes) are **not** testimonials for Multimedium. They may appear only inside screenshots of the client's site.
5. **Outcomes and metrics** appear only when the client confirms them and they have a source (analytics export, form counts, client statement). Store the source in a code comment next to the number.
6. **Disclose relationships.** PA Real Estate Support Services is Haydn's mom's business, so say so in its case study. Jennifer Cortes (Structured Closings) is also a Multimedium referral partner through My TC Academy. If her quote is used, add "Jennifer is also a Multimedium referral partner." ⚠️ Haydn to confirm wording. Disclosing a material connection is expected for endorsements.
7. **Client sites credit** ("Site by Multimedium") are fine, and Structured Closings already has one.

## 9. Visual principles ✅

- **Keep** the editorial foundation: warm paper background, deep navy ink, **one** blue accent, Manrope with Fraunces italic used once per page, hairline rules, and generous whitespace.
- **Remove** the lime accent, decorative asterisks, filler strips, and leftover glow/flood/ledger tokens.
- **Imagery** is real client work only, in one consistent browser/phone frame style, cropped so text is legible, with a one-line caption saying what the reader is looking for. **Haven Compass Realty leads the homepage.** No stock photos, AI imagery, or fictional brands. Use a new real founder photo when available.
- **Interaction:** subtle hovers, native `<details>` for FAQs, no parallax or motion libraries. Respect reduced motion.
- **Accessibility and performance stay as they are or improve:** axe-clean, visible focus, LCP < 2 s on throttled mobile.

## 10. Measurement
